Type

NEWCONCEPT

SECURITY

Logotype: NEW heavy (800) in black or white, CONCEPT regular (400) in NCS red, SECURITY regular and widely spaced. Montserrat is the closest web font.

Professional security, done properly.

Headings and text: the system sans (SF Pro on Apple, Segoe UI on Windows), as on newconceptsecurity.co.uk. Semibold for titles, regular for text.

Using the logo

  • Keep clear space around the logo at least the width of one side wedge.
  • The mark is NCS red on white or black, or all white on red and photographs. Black only for one colour print.
  • Never stretch, rotate, outline or recolour the mark, and never set NEWCONCEPT in one weight.
  • Smallest size: 24 px (6 mm) tall for the mark, 120 px (30 mm) wide for the full logo.
